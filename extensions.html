Extension methods

The right method makes all the difference.

There is no single “best” extension—only the best match for your hair, desired result and everyday life.

Hand-tiedK-tipsTape-insCompare
01

Flexible fullness

Hand-tied weft extensions

Fine wefts are secured along a carefully created beaded foundation, delivering lush volume with fewer attachment points.

  • Ideal for medium to thick hair
  • Excellent for fullness and dramatic length
  • Move-up maintenance typically every 6–8 weeks

Why guests love them: They create soft, blended volume and allow the hair to move as one beautiful, cohesive shape.

02

Undetectable versatility

K-tip extensions

Individual strands are attached with tiny keratin bonds, allowing precise placement and natural, 360-degree movement.

  • Works across many hair densities
  • Ideal for flexible styling and discreet placement
  • Worn for several months before a fresh installation

Why guests love them: The tiny bonds disappear into the hair and make ponytails, updos and free movement feel effortless.

03

Low-profile polish

Tape-in extensions

Slim, lightweight panels sit flat against the head to build silky length and volume with a streamlined application.

  • Suited to fine through medium density
  • Fast, comfortable installation
  • Move-up maintenance typically every 6–8 weeks

Why guests love them: They lie exceptionally flat and can create a smooth, polished result without unnecessary bulk.

Common questions

Hair extension FAQ

Will hair extensions damage my natural hair?

Professional assessment, correct weight distribution, careful installation and on-time maintenance are essential. Your stylist should only recommend a method your natural hair can safely support.

Can extensions be color matched?

Yes. We blend multiple shades and tones when needed to create dimension that flows naturally into your own hair.

How much do hair extensions cost?

Pricing depends on method, length, density, custom color and the amount of hair required. A consultation provides an accurate plan and quote.
